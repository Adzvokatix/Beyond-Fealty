## The square's path stepped with stone (his 1 and 2)

**What it was**: the path round a square is brought to the floor's level a quarter metre of its
length at a time, whole, where all of that quarter metre lies within half a metre of the level, and
left as the land lies where it does not; so where a levelled stretch ended the path stood two or
three blocks off it at once (272 such steps at the 323 squares of the ten places, 258 of them at a
levelled stretch's end: counted on 2026-11-21).

**The rule as built** (the spec, "The square's path stepped with stone"; `Set_Path_Steps`,
`--no-path-steps` is as it was; not saved; `Plan_Steps` in `Plan_Walls`; only of a square whose
walls stand outside its floor):

- From the end of each levelled stretch the path goes up or down to the land **a block a quarter
  metre**: the first quarter metre past the end is a step one block off the floor's level, toward
  the land's side of it, the next two, and so on while the path there lies at least that far off
  and every column of that quarter metre within half a metre of the step's row. The levelled
  stretch itself is not touched.
- **A step is of stone**: every block column of it across the path a tread of fieldstone at the
  step's row, **the wall builders' work**: columns of the walls' own plan, counted with the walls
  (`Count_Walls`), fetched for with them, laid in the same bouts (`Wall_Bout`).
- **Planned from the path as it is laid and from nothing else, each time** (nothing kept, as the
  walls): a quarter metre with a column still to lay, or under a tree, is no step and ends a flight
  until it is laid. So the steps do not go by who laid which metre first: the trial of 2026-11-21,
  which stepped the path as it was laid, did (below).
- **None where two sides' paths cross** (the square metre at each corner: laid by whichever side's
  metre comes first, and a tread there would leave the other side's metre unlevelled, `Lay_Metre`
  taking stone for ground it does not know).
- **No stair is moved, lost or made by them** (his 2: "The only thing left to fix on the square is
  the path jumps"). Whether a side has a stair is read from the path before its middle metre, each
  time (`Banks`: a bank of three blocks or more all across). So: before a stair that stands, no
  step; before the middle of a side with none, only steps one and two blocks off the floor's level,
  which no stair stands against.
- The walls and the strips beside a step are planned against the path as stepped, from the first.
- A step of the path is dug only with the stone for its tread in hand, and the tread laid in the
  same bout (dug and left, its top is earth, which no reading of the path knows, and the flight
  would be planned no more); whoever stands on a tread of stone of the path is settled on that
  tread (`Own_Ground`).
- A patch for every column (`Patch_Near`): the camp's square metres reach six and a half metres
  east and north of the fire, a walled side's path lies to 6.75 and 7; without it a step out there
  was counted as wanted and never gone to (the advisor's point; a check on ground rising to the
