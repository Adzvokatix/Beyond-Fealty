# Conventions: Buildings drawn turned along a path at 45 degrees

What past sessions learned and John ruled about this part, for whoever changes it. Read it with `docs/systems/rendering.md` ("A building drawn turned"), `docs/systems/habitation.md`.

Moved whole from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-22: every line is as it stood there, in the order it stood in. A line in italics says which entry the lines under it were parted from. *From the entry "His answers to those two rounds" (in `band.md`):*

  - **Then, the same evening** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_19_turned_building_and_workers.md`): "for the next couple of days we will be polishing, so hold off on the integration, we are goning to finish up the current projects, I am going to let you has it out ... can't we just \"detatch\" the entire building and rotate it? ... The questions can rest for now." **The network's later passes are held: build none before he says so. The calls rest: act on none.** A building at 45 degrees is to be **drawn as one turned piece** (his way, shown in a scratch still of a whole hovel: `~/CASTLE_GAME/releases/turned_hovel.png`, `turned_building_proof.patch`), not blocks cut corner to corner; the steps are in the brief ("Buildings at 45 degrees"), the first the draw, proved by a square house being today's picture to the pixel. A building's blocks are the map's own (walking, pointing, the cutaway, what is laid and saved): turned, they go into a grid of the house's own, with level ground and hidden wall blocks left on the map's grid.
  - **Step one of the turned building is built** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_19_turned_building_step_one.md`; `rendering.md`, "A building drawn turned"): `Castle.Render.Set_Turns` draws the blocks of timber, daub, thatch and plank in a box turned about a point, in the land's vertex shader (so it casts turned), lit by `nw` (the face as turned), cleared by `v_home` (where it stands on the grid); `--turn-houses` turns every hovel about its doorway, for looking. **The house's blocks stay the map's own, where it stands square on the grid; only the drawing is turned, about the doorway** (the one point where inside and outside meet with no jump): so the walking map, a stair, a second storey (his question: `Castle.Nav` keeps four floors over a spot already) need nothing new, and step two draws what is inside through the same turn. In the fragment shader `n` is the face on the grid (texels, corners, whatever goes by the block) and `nw` the face as lit: a new use of the normal picks the right one. A pixel proof of this renderer is "no more than the build differs from itself" (6 to 32 scattered pixels of a million with the proof's options), not zero.
  - **Step two is built** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_19_turned_building_step_two.md`): what is inside a house drawn turned goes through its turn. **A figure is turned at `Castle.Serfs.Submit`, after it is animated where it stands on the map** (`Turn_About`; never hand `Castle.Serfs` a turned place: it finds its own ground there); `Frames_Home` is the map's, `Frames_Of`, `Hand_At`, `Shoulders_Top`, `Log_Rest`, `Lying_Frame` and `Heading` the turned. Cloth is worked out on the map and drawn turned (`Put_Cloth`'s `Pivot`, `Radians`). A prop of a house's own goes through the game's `Put_In_House`: a new one does too. Still only `--turn-houses`; no house carries a turn until step three (the ground: next). `--clock-add H` for a still of sleepers by daylight.
  - **Step three, first half** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_19_turned_building_step_three.md`): a hovel beside a path at 45 degrees carries a turn (`CB.House.Turn`, set in `Place_House` by `Turn_For`; `House_View.Turn`; `Set_House_Turn`, `--no-house-turn`, held off in the reference runs, `stress.sh`, `look_cost.sh` and both `Reset`s; blob 36) and is drawn by it. The turn itself is drawing alone. A lot laid out is saved with its turn since blob 37 (before: `Load` works it out again by `Turn_For`).
  - **Step three, the ground** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_19_turned_building_ground.md`; on his "you're doing a great job, continue": praise, not a merge word). A hovel placed turned has `House.Turn_Ground` (fixed at placement, saved in the turn's byte as +4; `Set_Turn_Ground`, `--no-turn-ground`): the block columns under it as drawn and `Turn_Apron_B` round are of its site and its lot. **The turn's geometry is `Castle.Buildings.Back_Turn` / `Fore_Turn` / `Under_Drawn` / `Drawn_Box` and nowhere else** (the pivot is the middle of `Openings (1)` in the line of the front wall's middle, which is `Doorway_At (K, 1)`'s foot and half its width: the game's `Show_Turns` uses that; a turn to the left on the map is from U toward V only in a right-handed frame). **`In_Site` says which columns are a site's; `Site_U_Lo .. Site_U_Hi`, `Site_V_Lo .. Site_V_Hi` are only the box they lie in** (for a turned hovel the site is not a rectangle). The loops that do not go through `In_Site` were each given the turned ground by hand: `Lot_Take`, `Check_Lot`, `Choose_Pad`, `Work_In`'s boughs; `Join_Site` reaches to and from no turned-ground house. The blueprint's floor for such a house lies under it as drawn (so floor blocks lie outside the walls as they stand; `Lay_Door_Path` treads the way square to the path where there is no stair). `Set_Keep_Off` (`--no-keep-off`): `Keep_Off_Turned`, called every colony step, tells `Castle.Nav.Set_Kept` the ground of each standing turned-ground house less its walls' place and its door's way; `Castle.Nav.Build_Cell` (the one place a floor's speed is made) multiplies by `Kept_Slow`. **It slows, it never shuts**: the builder must stand there while building, and a shut cell could strand somebody. Nav's list is not saved: `Load` and `Reset` clear `Kept_Key`. **Prove a change here three ways**: `--no-keep-off` line for line the run before the keep-off; `--no-turn-ground --no-keep-off` line for line step three; `tools/turned_streets.sh` (in the gate since gate 60). `colony_run --turn-stats` is the measure (columns off level, grown over, who stood there). The mesher's shading along a wall's foot is cleared in the vertex shader for ground in a turn's box (`u_turn_all`, `v_ao`). `House_View.Drawn`, `Drawn_Door`: draw plans and outlines by these, not by `Corner`. Blob 37 (30 to 36 still read; a blob-36 house keeps its square ground). `colony_check` 811. The square the house stands on is turf to the eye and a wall to the feet.
  - **Step four, pointing and the cleared roof** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_19_turned_building_step_four.md`; the same word of his). `Castle.Pick.Look (..., Cast => ...)`: with the caster given (the game gives `Castle.World_Blocks.Cast'Access`, `pick_check` its own), each turned house is tried by the ray taken back through its turn (`House_View.Pivot`, `Turn`) and walked again from the box of the house as it stands; a hit counts only on a block of that house (row at or over its pad, `House_On` = it) and only if nothing seen stands before it. **`Thing.Turned`** says the block behind is not what is seen (the ray met a turned house, or walked into one where it stands): the game shows no brush box, digs nothing, and gives a tool's ground click `Under.Point`. `Seen_T` (the pad's level along the ray) stands in for `Ground.Dist` in that case, for sight and for the zone. Without `Cast` everything is as before (the checks that do not pass it). `Castle.Cutaway.Roof` has `Pivot`, `Turn_C`, `Turn_S` (`Of_Corners`' defaults: none); **`Home (R, P)` is the one place a seen point is taken back through a roof's turn**, used by `Follow`'s lines, `Wall_Cover`'s eye and `Castle.Render.Put_Roofs` (`u_roof_e`, a vec4 a roof: the eye as that house stands; for a house not turned it is the eye, and the picture is as before). The game's `Drawn_Person` goes through `Turned_In (I)`, which `Show_Members` fills. **`Sight` ends at `TL.T` when the ray meets a turned house** (no block stands there to stop the first walk: without it a person behind the house was pointed at through it). The game's `Drawn_Person` lays a sleeper as the colony does (`Castle.Pick.Colony_Person`), not by `Castle.Serfs.Heading`, which is 0 for a body that has not walked since a load. `Keep_Off_Turned`'s first box is the walls' own place exactly (a block more left a lane a cell wide inside the house as drawn). `pick_check` 69.
  - **A turned floor stepped by the texel, and turned hovels on hillsides** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_19_turned_floor_and_hillsides.md`; his words: "The textures are 5x5 on the face of each block right, can't we step the texture so it falls under the wall of the house? we don't want to limit the player they need to be able to place diagonals on hillsides if they want to"). **In the land's fragment shader `f_mat` is what a texel is drawn as; `v_mat` is what its block is** (everything after the texel `t` is found goes by `f_mat`): for a top face at a turned house's pad, the texel's middle is taken back through the turn and tried against `u_turn_walls` (the walls' outer faces as they stand, blocks: `Turn.Margin` in from the turn's box): inside is `u_turn_floor` (Footpath; -1 with `Castle.Render.Set_Turn_Floor (False)`, the game's `--no-turn-floor`), a floor block's texel up to 0.4 block outside is turf, and `refloored` keeps the turf fringe off both. `u_turn_all` is now the box every turn can be drawn in (its pivot and its reach), not the box of the boxes. The floor's *blocks* are where the blueprint lays them (to the walls' middle as drawn): do not move that raster without minding `Placed` in saves. **`Set_Turn_Fall` (`--turn-fall`) is off by default by his word**: `Check_Lot` takes the 2 m fall under a turned hovel's walls as they stand alone; the ground under it as drawn is made however far it falls. **Whatever `Work_In` asks for, the dig must do** (it asked for boughs over the drawn ground to be lopped and the lopping was done only over the house as it stands: a builder levelled for ever; the steep hillside in `tools/turned_streets.sh` is the guard). `In_A_House` holds the drawn place of a turned-ground house too. `colony_check` 813.
  - **His three answers, and the far view** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_20_far_view_and_answers.md`; "Ok lets continue,1. stopping at the outer face is preferable. 2. If you think bare earth would match better do it. 3. No limit is fine for now, we can adjust later"). The floor to the walls' outer faces stands; past them a floor block's texel is turf if `v_fr /= 0` (the mesher marked turf beside the block) else `u_bare`; the fall under a turned hovel as drawn stays unlimited. **A far tile's cells turn too**: the vertex shader's turn no longer asks `a_origin.w == 0`; for a tile a cell is asked by its middle and its top (`cs = 1 << a_origin.w`), and a building's cell whose foot is under the pad stays as turf clamped to the pad. **`Castle.Lod`'s mesher draws the top of a cell that a building's cell (Daub, Log, Thatch, Plank) stands on**, as `Castle.Mesher`'s `Under_Wall` does for stacks: without it the ground under turned-away walls is holes. The far view is drawn by distance from what the eye looks at (`u_focus`), not from the eye: to see it, look 240 m away from the thing from high up (`$S/pixfar.sh`). Step five, the stockyard beside a path at 45 degrees, was put to him as three ways and built on his word (the next entry but one). Owed: a faint line in the far view where a turned house stands (cause not found).
  - **A stockyard beside a path at 45 degrees is laid out along the path** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_20_stockyard_along_a_path.md`; his word, of three ways put to him: "Ok go on with the second way for the stockyard"). `Set_Yard_Turn` (`--no-yard-turn`; saved, blob 39 with `House.Piv_U`, `Piv_V` for a storehouse; 30 to 38 still read). **Two frames to keep apart, of a storehouse with `Castle.Buildings.Yard_Turned` (a storehouse with `Turn /= 0`)**: its *frame* (`H_X`, `H_U`, `H_Point`: where its own blocks stand, square to the grid, as ever) and its *design* (`Yard_U0` .., `Front`, the way in, the gateways, the coverings, a place's fitting: all said in blocks as a square stockyard's are, and made at `Fore_Turn` of them). `CB.Design_Of` / the colony's `D_U`, `D_V` give the design place of a frame column (every column has one; a design place has one column, two or none, so **a piece of ground is gone over by the frame's columns and asked of the design, never the other way**; a line or a spot goes design to world by `Y_Point`, `Y_X`, `Y_Y`). `In_Site`, `Level_Of`, `In_Door_Way`, `Is_Stair` and `Patch_Of` take a frame column and look the design up (their `_D` forms take the design's); `Site_U_Lo` .. are the frame's box of it (`Yard_Box`). `Check_Lot`, `Choose_Pad`, `Lot_Take` and `Lay_Door_Path` have a branch for it. The blueprint (`Castle.Buildings.Blueprint`) rasterises the fence and the gateways' planks as lines of columns each beside the one before (`Add_Col`; a diagonal step gets the column between), samples the beaten ground and the coverings by frame column, and leaves out any yard block that would stand among the storehouse's own (`Yard_Clashes`, which `Place_Storehouse_Turned` requires to be 0; the plank's end under the eave is emitted as one of its own blocks, in the frame, and so is drawn at the corner as drawn). **The feet go by it as drawn once its walls and roof are up** (`Walked_Drawn`: `Placed >= Dress_N`; `Walk_Drawn` each tick and at the end of `Load` gives `Castle.Nav.Set_Drawn` each such storehouse's walls as drawn, a four-cornered piece each with the doorways left out, and the box its blocks stand in): `Castle.Nav.Build_Cell` gives a cell that overlaps a wall piece no floor and any other cell that overlaps the box one floor at the pad's row. **That box is the one place where the walking map is not read from the blocks**; within the walls as drawn but off the box it is read from them as ever. When the list changes every route's stamps are zeroed, as a load leaves them (looked over step by step at the next check): do not set `Replan` there, a run saved in that tick and loaded would part from the straight one. `House_View.Yard_Turned`, `Walked_Drawn`, `People_Turned` (a hovel's: always; such a storehouse's: only while `Floor_N <= Placed < Dress_N`); the game turns people only where `People_Turned` and eases the storehouse builder's turn over `Ease_M` beyond the ring (`Turned_Part`); a worn cloak turns with its wearer. `Render.Turn.Floor` is each turn's floor stuff (`u_turn_mat`); the storehouse's box is its walls' outer faces and 0.25 m (its eaves, to the block). `Castle.Pick`: the ground within such a storehouse's walls as they stand is `Unseen` at the pad's own row too (not the cursor's). `Raise_Now` lays a turned yard by `Yard_Row`, as `Step_House` does; a square one's is written as drawn up, as before. `colony_check` 833, `path_check` 84, `pick_check` 78. **A lot at 45 degrees is given by its two opposite corners along and across the path's way**: two corners straight along it or straight across it are a strip a metre wide (`tools/yard_corners.py` works them out; the game's `--pointer` has `tool NAME` and `follow I`).
  - **Turned hovels walked where they are drawn, and a stockyard's pieces** (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_20_walked_hovels_and_yard_pieces.md`; his words: "Let's do that, and what do you think about turning the fence too? The outdoor roof covering needs a smoothing pass"). `Set_Hovel_Walk` (`--no-hovel-walk`; `House.Walk`, fixed at placement, +8 in the turn byte; blob 40): a turned hovel is on `Walk_Drawn`'s list once its walls and roof are up; its bed (`Bed_Place`, `House_Inward`) and whoever is in it are where it is drawn; nothing is kept off it. **A hovel's walls as drawn slow, they do not shut** (`Castle.Nav.Drawn_House.Slow`: floors at `Wall_Speed`, a step onto or off one reckoned at `Wall_Pace` in `Step_Time` and in `Line`): shut, the ground made round a hovel on a hillside held pockets a serf could drop into and not climb out of; **make a change to the walking map's drawn walls on `colony_check`'s hillside streets, the steep one too, and count the pits**, and read `turned_streets.sh` (it fails on anyone in a wall as drawn, or a bed not got to by its door). **A pace multiplied into a floor does not keep a way off it** (a step's pace is the mean of its two floors', a twentieth at the least): a cost has to be in `Step_Time` and `Line` both, or a way planned round is straightened through. A doorway a metre wide at 45 degrees needs its cells held open (`Door` pieces). Walls are asked of every building before any box; a hovel's box makes only cells wholly in it level. `Set_Smooth_Covers` (`--no-smooth-covers`) and `Set_Fence_Pieces` (`--no-fence-pieces`; `House.Smooth_Covers`, `.Fence_Pieces`, said when a storehouse is begun; blob 41): the blueprint's stage `Smooth` (last; work alone, `Kind_Of`) lays each covering's thatch again with the state `Castle.Blocks.Piece`; a turned yard's rails and gateway planks are laid with it. **A block of state `Piece` (log, plank, thatch) is left out of what the land draws** (`Castle.Blocks.Seen_Mat`, in `Castle.Mesher`, `Castle.Lod` and the rim of a neighbour's edits `Castle.Edits` hands the mesher: without the rim a post beside a hidden rail across a stack's edge lost a face) and drawn as a prop instead (`Castle.Colony.Yard_Pieces`, the game's `Show_Yard_Pieces`); to everything else it is the block it is. **A yard stage that lays a block again where one stands must say so in `Yard_Row`** (it skips anything standing there: the first smoothing laid nothing). **A view kept per house must not be kept while its ground is not in** (the game's first frames: `Yard_Pieces` drew every rail underground until it was not kept so). A prop's frame must be right-handed (its X, its top's normal, its slope), or it draws the box's insides; a prop bigger than a metre or so is one box sized to it if its net fits the skin (tiles showed a seam at every edge from afar). The test world keeps a block's state. **Before calling a look done, watch it being built** (a still half way, and the game run live through it), not only a finished one loaded at speed 0; and take every proof that nothing else moved again with the final build. `colony_check` 845, `path_check` 88.
