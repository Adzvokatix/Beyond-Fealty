# Conventions: The interface

What past sessions learned and John ruled about this part, for whoever changes it. Read it with `docs/systems/interface.md`.

Moved whole from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-22: every line is as it stood there, in the order it stood in. A line in italics says which entry the lines under it were parted from. Where an entry says "on branch `stockpile`, not merged", read "merged 2026-11-22". New rules of this part are added here, at the top, dated.

- **A person's menu as it is today is the development interface, and any job may be given there** (John, 2026-11-22: "The cook is earned by skill, but the development UI is the backend we can assign jobs however we want with the development UI, the player won't be able to."). So a job on that menu is not a ruling that the player gives it. By his design document the player gives four (hunter, gatherer, porter, woodcutter) and the others are come to by skill; the player's own interface, when it is built, is held to that. What goes on the development menu is ours to choose. **Built on his word of the same day** (asked whether to move Cook to the development entries and add the stockman and the carpenter: "yes add the cook and the stockman and carpenter"): the development entries are Cook (in its old row), Builder, Stockman and Carpenter (`Castle.Actions.Dev_Jobs`; `docs/systems/interface.md`); `--no-dev-jobs`, which stands for the player's menu, gives no job and the four, and no Cook. **A job worked in goes on that list in the same piece of work** (his rule of 2026-11-19, "keep adding new jobs as we work them in to that list": the stockman and the carpenter had been worked in on 2026-11-17 and left off it). **A trade named by hand is kept in what the colony already saves** (who the stockman and the carpenter are: no blob bump), is nobody's trade until its building stands, and one named a carpenter that took a house to build meanwhile leaves it when the storehouse stands: a new trade's entry needs the same three answers. `colony_check`'s older sections describe the player's menu (six rows) with the development entries held off in `Reset`.

- Designer's rules for stage 5a (2026-11-10): a right click on the ground opens its menu (zone here, path from here) instead of ending a path line; a line is ended from the menu or with Esc, which cancels what is on the go before it quits. In the development view a tooltip shows everything in a person's view, moods included; the view marks what the player's character could know, for the game's own tooltips later. One stockpile zoning is one zone; two that touch stay two. Deer are pickable and get a tooltip (class, herd size, calm or alarmed) but no actions yet. The development panels stay on Tab and the F-keys, and nothing of the game's interface depends on them (hide them all with F4 and tooltips and menus still work).
