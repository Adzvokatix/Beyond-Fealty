- **2026-11-23, later: a stockpile is one level** (his "flatten the stockpile, let's just make this easy for ourselves"; `docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_23_a_stockpile_is_one_level.md`; `docs/systems/colony.md`, "A stockpile is one level"; `Set_Pile_Flat`, off by default since that afternoon: `--pile-flat`). With the rule, where a stockpile is put down all its ground is brought at once to the level of its path side, whatever the land is made of, and it takes all of its box. For whoever changes it:
  - **The level is its ground's beside its path, not its middle's.** Counted over 58 stockpiles before building: at the middle of all its ground four have no part of their front within a step of their path, his own among them. A level is tried by `colony_run --flat-scan` with the rule off, which says for four levels the cut, the fill and how much of the front can be walked into.
  - **What is "left as it lies" is not left for good: the band and the cart change it.** A boulder left standing was prised away by the wall-layers and the earth under it stood bare ten blocks over the floor; a boulder's top left in the floor was prised out of it; the ground under the cart stayed as the land lay after the cart was drawn off, a hollow a serf does not climb out of. Each kind of column the rule leaves (`Flat_Column`) has to say who looks at it again and when: a tree's, when it is felled; the cart's, while it is in tow; and the kinds left for good close their places. A day after anything, `--flat-scan` must say none "to move still".
  - **The land is cut whatever it is made of**, and **no block of a boulder lies in a floor's top** (`Flat_Top`). Bog iron lies in the turf of three block columns in ten at the reference stake: a rule that moves earth alone leaves a third of a meadow.
  - **A bush is lifted off before its island's top is read**, its stack looked through for bushes first (a stockpile put down with the stake comes before the stake's stacks are indexed). The first run of the rule starved a gatherer in a pit by a bush at the square in the wood.
  - **The cart's bed is blocks**: the block columns it stands in are no zone's, no place is planned where it stands, and a block column it stands on is asked of before anything else (`Flat_Kind`: its bed "stands where the earth would go", which would close the place for good). Nothing of the cart is touched (his word of 2026-11-22); where it comes to stand in a stockpile can differ, since its own rule takes the nearest two free places its bed fits on and on a level floor a nearer pair fits (three metres off, in the game as it is played).
  - **A stockpile's lot is planned for the ground it will have** (`Plan_Lot`, `One_Level`), or a stockpile on a hillside has flat floor with no places on it where the land was steep.
  - **The runner's measures have one list of what is ground** (`Is_Land` in `tools/colony_run.adb`), the land whatever it is made of. Each had its own, none with ore or a boulder in it: 27 of 29 places at the reference stake read as a block under their level and none was.
  - **Not tried: live water beside a deep cut** (a stockpile below a tarn). What was run: a made-up pond of water blocks keeps its water and its bank; a stockpile dragged down to a river's bank in the game leaves the river at its level, with the rule and without; a pond the game poured into a pit (`--puddle`, with `--late`) is cut away with its pit by a stockpile put down over it and its water is gone, where with the rule off the stockpile is zoned round the pit (93 places for 99) and the pond stays. **And not tried: a block column more than `Flat_Most_B` off its level** (none at 58 stockpiles).
