  - **A zone is its ground**, strips of block columns (`Zone_Run_At`), and **one lookup says whose a block column is**: `Zone_Of_Block` in the colony's body (`Zone_On`, `Zone_Top`, `On_Path` outside it): a zone's, else a path's, else nobody's. Zoning, `Lay_Path`, `Castle.Pick` and the drawing all ask it (done-test: nothing else tests a cell's square or the camp's square for "is this zoned").
  - **One routine lays out a lot**: `Plan_Lot` (fill from a start, snap to a path within `Snap_M`, terminate at paths, zones and ledges). `Plan_Stockpile` and `Plan_Camp` show what a zoning would take with nothing changed, and the tools draw that plan (`Castle.Zone_Marks.Show_Plan`): a preview is never worked out separately. A new kind of zone lays its lot through `Plan_Lot`.
  - **Old lots give the old cells.** A stockpile on whole metres beside a path that runs east or north gets the places it always got, in the same order, and only the places' grass is cut (the rest of a zone's ground keeps its grass for that reason: cutting it moved the reference logs). `tools/reference_check.sh` proves it; don't "tidy" either without re-recording the logs and saying so.
  - **The band's labour** is one switch, `Set_Labour` (`--no-labour` off; `--instant-camp` was its first name and is still taken): on, the band builds the camp and lays the paths (`Think_Build`) and the fire is tended by hand, eight hours a log and three of dying down; off, the camp stands and a path is laid when marked, and the fire is the old one. `colony_check` runs its older sections with it off (`Set_Up (With_Labour => False)`); the three older reference logs run with `--no-labour`, the two after them with `--all-hands --no-level` (the camp and zones log with `--paths-built` as well), `reference_run_hands_level.txt` is the run with all of that on, and all six run with `--deer-scatter` since 2026-11-11 (`reference_run_herds_flight.txt` is the run with everything on).
  - **What is built on a zone** is on the zone record (`Built`, `Got`) against `Build_Takes (kind)`; what it still takes goes into `Build_Rows` as the zone's `Once` rows. The camp's hearth is the first user; a storehouse is a row of `Build_Takes` and a `Think_Build` of its own, not new globals.
  - **Wood on the fire goes through `Feed_Fire`** (nothing burns where no hearth stands); `Fire_Wants` is the one test of whether it is to be stoked. Logs are carried only by the cook and a forester (`Carries_Logs`; the 2026-11-08 decision), never by a porter.
  - `--camp-built` / `Build_Camp_Now` and `--stoke` build the camp on the spot for a scripted start; a shot or a check that wants a fire needs one of them.

- Extra hands, stretches and the camp's floor (2026-11-11; `docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_11_hands_and_level.md` has his words, the rules as built and his calls). Closed as built: first values, his to tune; don't treat one as settled against him, and don't change one unasked. The calls put to him stand by default (the handoff lists them). In the code:
  - **Who is at an extra task is read, never kept**: `At_Extra_Task (J)` from what J is doing (a patch, a metre, an errand for the hearth, earth in hand, between two metres of its stretch). `Has_Place` in `Think_Build` is the one gate (a job and fewer than `Extra_Hands` at it; no job: always; the cook at its own camp: always, and not counted). Done-test: a new extra task adds its states to `At_Extra_Task` and asks `Place`; `colony_check` looks at the limit every twentieth of a second through a night and through a levelling. A load already in hand is never gated.
  - **A stretch is held by being at it**: `Site_Seg` while the member is in `To_Patch`/`Tamping`, or `Idle` between two metres. Anything else it turns to drops it (`Drop_Stretch`: in `Release`, when the needs take it in `Think`, at the end of `Think_Build`). Its turn ends in `Think_Build` and it goes on to its own work in that same `Think`: don't end it in `Step_Build`, or it takes the next stretch at once.
  - **The camp's ground is read again after every dig or fill** (`Read_Camp_Ground`: the zone's strips remade, `Zones_Version` moved on), since `Runs.Z` is what the patches, the marks and the pick trust. Anything else that changes a zone's ground must do the same for that zone.
  - **Earth for a hollow is always there by how the level is chosen** (`Choose_Level`: dug earth plus the turf's, `Sod_Share`). The turf's earth is the one thing that comes from nothing; it is used only after the digging and never carried. Don't make the level "the fire's own height" or "the lowest": the first leaves hollows nobody can fill on day one, the second digs four times as much.
  - **No fetching to and fro**: earth is fetched from the stockpile only when nothing is left to dig, a hollow is free and not failed for that member, and it has not lately found none it could get to (`Hollow_Fail`, ten game hours). This is the same trap as the cook's log at an unbuilt camp: an errand that takes from the stores must first know it can deliver.
  - Nav cells are half a metre and take the highest of their four block columns, so `Castle.Nav.Surface_Z` at the floor's rim is the bank's height, not the floor's: compare a zone's ground with the blocks, not with nav (`On_Ground` gives the floor's level on a camp that stands).
  - `--all-hands` (no limit, no stretches) and `--no-level` are the switches that keep the two labour-on reference logs; `--extra-hands N` sets the limit.
