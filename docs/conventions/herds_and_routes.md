- **2026-11-23: a walker is at the height of the place it steps into as it steps into it** (`Castle.Colony.Set_Step_At_Edge`, `--no-step-at-edge`, in the colony's `Walk`; `docs/systems/routes.md`; `docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_23_paths_builders_and_stockpiles.md`, section 9), on branch `playtest-notes-2`, not merged. On for everyone who walks; held off in the reference runs, `stress.sh`, `look_cost.sh` and `colony_check`'s `Reset`. To keep: **a way's point where the height changes is not at the edge but up to an eighth of a metre past it** (`Castle.Nav.Line` puts it at the first sample inside the next cell), **and a walker stops at the same distance from the same work every time**, so a stop "now and then" in that eighth is a stop there every visit; **a walker's height between two points is the colony's to say** (`castle-colony-walk.adb`), not the map's; a cell's floor is its highest block column, so one standing at its floor is never in the ground, and whoever is, stopped between two points or had the ground moved under it. **The measure is the whole of `colony_run --builder-stats`'s "in the ground"**, by what each was doing and where (`--builder-list N`), and `--feet-stats`; `--watch MEMBER S0 S1` says one member second by second. I read one part of it (a builder making ground ready) for a day and missed 2,540 serf-seconds of people standing in a step at the stockpile, five times what the game had before the round (517; 703 with the places made level taken out of the round, 238 with that and the cart's room). **A rule that moves ground where people stand is measured with the whole count before and after.**

- **2026-11-22: a herd is put down only where a deer can walk off** (`Castle.Herds.Set_Walk_Off`, `--herds-anywhere`; John: "get stuck in the tree if they spawn in inside a tree?"; `docs/systems/deer.md`, "A herd is not put up a tree"). **`Castle.Nav.Surface_Z` is the highest floor at a place, and a tree's trunk two blocks thick has one on its top**: anything put down by it (a herd, a stake in a scan) can land up a tree. Ask whether what is put there can walk off (`Castle.Herds.Can_Walk_Off`), or take the floor nearest a height known to be the ground's. **A fault of where things are put is reproduced by a count over many stakes** (`colony_run --herd-scan R STEP`: 14 of 800 herds on a trunk's top), judged against something the walking map did not say (the generator's own height of the land, the block under the floor). The count found a second thing and it is left: deer stepping down onto mountain ledges they cannot step up from (17 of 4,000 an hour on). **A rule for where a thing is put asks nothing of the planner and draws no number**, so that every run in which the old place was good is the very same run: the eight kept logs and the played game's were line for line with it on, none recorded again.

- What routing and the colony's steps cost (2026-11-11; `docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_11_pathing.md` has his words, the design note he pasted, what was measured, built and not built, and his calls; `docs/systems/routes.md`, "What routing costs"). Accepted as built: on `main`. First values, his to tune; don't treat one as settled against him, and don't change one unasked. In the code:
  - **Measure before building anything for speed**: `colony_run --nav-stats` (the seconds in the colony's steps, the walking map's reads, every plan, the twelve slowest with where from and where to) and `perf record -F 999 -o FILE -- ./bin/colony_run ...` (flat profiles only on this machine; `perf report --sort pid`, then `--tid N --sort symbol` for the main thread). The note's two suspects were 2 % of the time and already done; the hogs were elsewhere. The two measuring runs are the reference band with its orders through 41 days and `--band 48 --gatherers 14 --foresters 6 --porters 8 --hunters 8 --cooks 2` through 12.
  - **What is only kept to save work must change no run, and has a switch that proves it**: the walking map mended (`Castle.Nav.Mending`, `--no-mending`), what a search for a source found (`Castle.Colony.Set_Source_Memos`, `--no-memos`). `tools/stress.sh runner` runs the reference band both ways and compares the states. A new cache gets its switch and its line there; it is never saved and nothing may be decided by whether it is there.
  - **An edit tells `Castle.World_Blocks` at once**: `Note_Edit` (which column: `Block_At` asks the edit store only about edited columns) and `Reground` (the column's ground read again, the stack's version moved, the column logged for `Changes_Since`). The game's and the runner's write paths do both; anything else that changes blocks must, or stamp the whole stack as `Keep` and `Drop` do. Everything kept by a ground version (the walking map, the source memos, routes) trusts that no block changes without the version moving.
  - **The walking map is mended** (`Castle.Nav.Stack`: only the cells over the columns `Changes_Since` names are read again, and the pieces of ground are labelled again only if a cell changed). The debug build reads every mended stack whole beside it and compares (`Check_Mended`): a change to what a cell is made from (`Read_Whole`) needs nothing more, a change to which columns a cell depends on does. `path_check` and `colony_check` have no edit log (their worlds are made up), so they read whole.
  - **A goal out of reach is always answered by the pieces of ground** (`Gather` maps or mends a stale stack on its way: `Castle.Nav.Map_On_The_Way`), never by searching everything in reach. That moved where some routes end, so the seven reference logs were regenerated (c8e1c4a) and the first winter comes out otherwise (all six live with a forester, two die with none; before it one and none). Off gives the old runs back, with a 55 ms plan about once a game day; don't turn it off, and don't compare a run with a state or a log from before c8e1c4a.
  - **A source memo is kept by the ground versions of its stack and the four beside it** (`Source_Memo` in the colony's body, `Read_Stack` in `Find_Source`). `Source_At` must stay a function of the blocks alone, within one block column of the place: if a source ever depends on something else (a season, who owns it), that goes in `Consider`, which is asked every time, or in the memo's key.
  - **Not built, and why** (routes.md has the numbers): routes are already walked again and kept when a stack they cross changes (`Still_Good`); the route store, the anchors and the coarse graph wait for stage 6's buildings to anchor to or for dozens of people (the long way is 17 ms for six, 28 ms for forty-eight); a herd's dry way still searched to its budget (known out of reach and refused since 2026-11-13: the herds-search entry); `Held` adds up the stores for every member every think (a tenth of forty-eight's time).

- Designer's rules for stage 3 (2026-11-08): herds follow a node (see Architecture); the hunter's starting weapon is the **short bow**, and the sling is dropped from the game. Hunters go after the nearest grown deer of a herd larger than `Spare_Last` (3) only while meat is short of its reserve and deer aren't spared; they stalk, shoot within `Shoot_M` (18 m) with a clear line, carry the kill home and butcher it at the stockpile into meat and a hide.
