# Conventions: Saves, the checks, the reference runs

What past sessions learned and John ruled about this part, for whoever changes it. Read it with `docs/systems/edits_and_saves.md`, `docs/systems/switches.md`, `tools/gate.sh`.

Moved whole from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-22: every line is as it stood there, in the order it stood in. A line in italics says which entry the lines under it were parted from. Where an entry says "on branch `stockpile`, not merged", read "merged 2026-11-22". New rules of this part are added here, at the top, dated.

- **2026-11-23, later: a guard proved by cutting it may be one of two.** The check of a tree on a knoll in a stockpile made one level passed with the mend taken out: the pillar under the felled tree is cut by the stockpile made level again at the felling, and, a second later, by the tree's own place being made level. A check that passes with the fix cut is looked at for a second thing doing the same work, and then given a line that has the one alone (that check's second line is run with `Set_Place_Level` off; its comment says which line fails with what cut). **And a measure's own list of what counts is the code's** (`Is_Land`, `docs/conventions/stores.md`): a figure that reads the same with a rule on and off (27 of 29 places "a block low" at the reference stake) is a reading of the measure, not of the ground.

- **2026-11-23: a save is tried with the new thing finished, not only half built, and in the game as it is played.** The fuller truss put a flag a house at the end of the blob (45); `Load` held what was laid of each house against its blueprint where it read the house, before the flag: a hovel standing with the fuller truss has more blocks laid than the blueprint of before holds, and **every save with such a hovel was refused by the build that made it** ("no colony in that save"), through five commits and a gate that passed. The checks saved a hovel at its frame; the site scripts save squares and stockpiles; nothing saved a colony with a new hovel standing. Since then `tools/today_check.sh` saves the game as it is played on its twentieth day, loads it and runs it on to the straight run's state, in the gate. To keep: **what a load reads late in the blob is not known where the thing is read**: anything that depends on it (a count, a test against a count) is made after it is read, and a new field of a house, a zone or a member is tried by a save of the played game with that thing in every state it can be in. Whole `colony_check` before a blob's commit (its checks of older blobs cut bytes off a new blob's end).

- **2026-11-22, the same day, later: a log is read for who lived before it is kept.** The log of the game as it is played, recorded that morning for its cart, has one of the thirteen stand in a pocket of ground at a hovel's lot from day 26 and die of hunger on day 46 (`docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_22_playtest_notes.md`, "First"); it went into the gate unread, and `tools/today_check.sh` compares lines: it says nothing of what the lines say. **Before a run is kept as a reference: `grep` it for "died", "starving" and "can't get there", and say what is found in the commit that keeps it.** The three site scripts fail on a death or an empty belly by themselves; this one does not. The log stands as recorded (it is what `main` does) until the pocket is mended on his word; then it is recorded again.

- **2026-11-22: the game as it is played has a kept log of its own** (`tools/today_run.sh`, `tools/today_check.sh`, `docs/production/reference_runs/today_41_days.txt`; in the gate). The eight reference logs are a band of six with every rule since 2026-11-17 held off (`docs/systems/switches.md`): none founds a square, puts down a stockyard or turns a building, so "the eight logs hold" said nothing of what John plays. This run is every rule on: thirteen with jobs on a square at 1238 1058, a stockpile and the stockyard put down whole, housing pulled eastward, 41 days with `--who` and `--box-stats`. **A change that is not about what the game does leaves it passing with nothing recorded again. A new rule on by default makes it fail: first show that it passes with the new rule's switch off (`tools/today_check.sh --no-the-rule`), then record it again in the rule's own commit (`--record`) and say in the commit what moved.** A blob bump moves its state's hash alone, which the script reports apart. (The run's housing lies east of the square because with the housing pulled westward from 1231 1058 in its place the colony had no cart at the end of 41 days, 35 deer unsalted and 1,510 lb of meat lost; likewise from 1224 1050 after two days; with four other pulls, and with none, it had its cart. *From the entry "His answer of 2026-11-21" (in `square.md`):*

  - **`colony_run` leaves with 1 when it raises** (a handler at the end of its body: what it raised, the traceback, `OS_Exit`): left to the run-time it said so and then stood for ever, waiting for the workers' tasks, and a gate with it. A new tool that starts `Castle.Streaming`'s workers ends the same way.
  - **What a save leaves out the loader must make again exactly, and a run on from a save says whether it does** (2026-11-21: of 62 saves taken half way, five did not run on to the straight run's state, from three causes, two mended). **A lot still to begin is saved as its place alone and its house designed anew from its seed: whatever is given a design after `CB.Design` goes in `Give_Timbers`**, which `Place_House` and the loader both call (the loader left the timbers out, and every house begun after a load from a lot laid out before it was built without its roof's timbers, its windows' timber and its ridge beam: the "colony with housing saved in its first days" fault; `Lot_Design` and a check hold every lot the same design, whole, across a save). **A reservation is not saved: the loader gives it back by the field it is taken and let go with** (`M.Tree`, in every state: an apple tree being picked was nobody's after a load), not by a list of states. **Still owed, each needing more in the blob**: a route that ends short (`Partial`) is planned anew at its first look after a load, where a straight run keeps it until its ground changes (`yh3/new_4.sav`: a forester going for a log on a bough); of two pickers at one bush the loader makes the later of the band its holder. **To find why a run on parts**: `--log-hours 0.05 --who` in both, the first block that differs (the loaded run's first block is one later than the straight run's); then the cause tried alone in a scratch build, and every kept save run on in it (`$S/runon_all2.sh`). A scratch script copied for another tree by `sed` must be read for the old tree's name before a pass of it is believed (a cut copy "passed" on the uncut tree's binary).
  - `colony_run --square-scan` founds colony after colony in one world: whatever one leaves there stands in the next one's way (the cart's bed did; `Take_Cart_Away`). A save for a run-on ends on a multiple of thirty colony seconds (the runner says when it does not).
