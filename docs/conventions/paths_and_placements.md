# Conventions: Paths, and zones put down whole

What past sessions learned and John ruled about this part, for whoever changes it. Read it with `docs/systems/colony.md` ("placements"), `docs/systems/routes.md`.

Moved whole from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-22: every line is as it stood there, in the order it stood in. A line in italics says which entry the lines under it were parted from. Where an entry says "on branch `stockpile`, not merged", read "merged 2026-11-22". New rules of this part are added here, at the top, dated.

- **2026-11-23: where trodden ground meets trodden ground** (his "we need to figure out a way to handle how paths and zone buildings meet up, we have blocks that aren't knocked down as you can see in some of the photos, we have the .5m jumps where a path is extended from square"; `docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_23_paths_builders_and_stockpiles.md`, section 4). Two rules of mine, each a first answer and his to judge, both on, neither saved, both held off in the reference runs and in `colony_check`'s `Reset`: **a path is stepped where it meets trodden ground** (`Step_Seams`, called at the end of `Lay_Metre` and of `Lay_Door_Path`; `Set_Paths_Meet`, `--no-paths-meet`) and **a door's way is made again to its path as it lies** (`Meet_Door`, called when a hovel's ground is ready and when it stands; `Set_Door_Meet`, `--no-door-meet`). To keep: **who gives way is fixed**: the square's own path, the strips kept for its walls and any zone's ground are never moved by `Step_Seams` (a floor and a landing are where they are planned; a path adapts to the square, a door's way to its path); **a column is moved a block at a time toward what it meets, two at the most at one laying, two metres round what was laid**: so it cannot dig a pit (every move brings two trodden columns nearer one another) and a steep bank keeps what cannot be stepped within that; the count is `colony_run --seam-stats` (a jump: two blocks; a step: one), and `--ground-map` draws a place to read. **Not mended, counted**: a hovel that fronts the square's own path where that path was brought two blocks or more off the hovel's floor after the hovel was placed ends its way half a metre off it (8 pairs of block columns at two of eight layouts): the placement would have to plan against the square's path as it will lie. **The strip of turf in his pictures 9 and 10 was not made to happen**: read as a step of a door's way left standing over a path that came to lie lower, which is what `Meet_Door` cuts; said so to him.

- **2026-11-23: a zone's strips do not lie in the list in the zones' order.** `Read_Zone_Ground` and `Unzone_Box` take a zone's strips out of `Runs` and put the new ones on the end, so the square (zone 1) has its strips last from the moment its floor is made. `Join_Plan` packed every zone's strips again in the zones' order, **in place**: the square's were written over the stockpile's before those were moved, and every zone after the first one out of order had another zone's ground for its own (his "the stockpile zones just disappeared at some point": the places and what lay in them stayed, the ground under them was nobody's, nothing was drawn round it). It packs from a copy of the list now. **Anything new that moves strips in `Runs` reads `First` and never assumes an order**; `colony_check`'s "zones keep their ground when housing is joined to housing" fails with the packing in place put back. Made to happen by an order given days into a run (`colony_run --later HOURS ORDER ...`: `docs/systems/colony.md`, "colony_run"), which no kept log had: **a fault of "at some point" is looked for with a second order given later, in one run** (a save puts the strips back in order, and a run loaded from it does not show it).

*From the entry "Foresters with no tree in reach, and paths straight or at 45 degrees" (in `band.md`):*

  - **A path is ordered straight or at 45 degrees** (`Castle.Actions.Path_Snap`, `Path_Begin`, `Path_Reach`; `Castle.Colony.Path_Line_Start`, `Path_Line_End`; `--no-path-snap`; not saved): `Perform` applies them, and whoever shows a line before ordering it shows it by the same two (the HUD's path tool, the game's `Show_Path_Line`), **which give back such a point as it is**: keep that so, or the line shown and the line laid part and a chained line drifts. `Castle.Colony.Lay_Path` lays any line (`colony_check`'s paths at every angle, `stockyard_sites.sh`'s first site); `colony_check`'s `Reset` holds `Path_Snap` off. A stretch's joints on a long diagonal are not on block corners (`Line_Piece`): judge whether a path lies on the grid by `Grid_Way` (its way and its middle line), never by its ends.
  - **Not mended, found on the way**: a path drawn into a river has a last metre nobody can lay, and the way to it is searched for again and again (45 ms each); others of the band ask the way to things across a river thousands of times (`colony_run --nav-stats` at stake 1000 900).

*From the entry "His answers of 2026-11-22, later" (in `square.md`):*

  - **A planned line's point under a tree has the ground's height** (`Line_Point`; `Set_Line_Ground`, `--no-line-ground`; on in `colony_check` too): where what is stood on there is no ground, the highest ground under it by `Top_By`. The eight reference logs hold with it.

*From the entry "His answer of 2026-11-21" (in `square.md`):*

  - **A path at 45 degrees that anything is to lie along runs on every other diagonal of the block grid, corner of blocks to corner of blocks**: shift one by a single diagonal row and a stockpile held by it is refused or lays a path of its own beside it. So an angled square's strip is two rings or none.

- His design for placing zones (2026-11-20; `docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_20_placements_with_paths_and_the_square.md` has his words whole, mapped clause by clause, his answers to the four calls, what is built and what is not), on branch `stockpile`, not merged. **He took the first of the two ways for building at an angle (what is built on an angled zone kept in a grid of its own), with the placed zone as the thing that has the grid: so his items "hovel walls solid" and "turn the fence posts" are not built the present way; they come with the zone's own grid, which is sized in the brief and not begun.** His rulings, to keep: **an angled zone's ground is the map's quarter-metre blocks that fit within it, made level a square metre at a time** (so its edge on the ground steps by the block, and that is no fault); **a placed box over anything is refused with its reason shown, housing joins housing as it does**; the square's path lies round the outside of its 10 m; **the lots' polish waits until all of this is through**. In the code:
  - **`Castle.Colony.Box_At (Kind, Middle, Way)` is the one routine that says where a held box lies** (spec, "placements"); `Plan_Box` shows it and `Place_Box` places it by that same routine, and held by its own middle a box must come back as it is (`colony_check` asks it at every way and every snap): a new way of drawing a box to something is a new `Offer` there, and must give itself back. **Where a box's path meets another path's line it is where the box's own line crosses it, never the foot of the box's end on it** (the foot crept a block along the path each time it was held again, aslant).
  - **A box is its path and the ground on the left of it**: the path from a corner of blocks a whole number of blocks along one of the eight ways (`Way_Index`; 29 slanting blocks for 10 m, so that a slanting stockpile has its hundred places), the box from the path's edge back. Its lot is planned by `Plan_Lot` with its own path put into `Path_List` for the length of the plan (so the zone's ground stops at it), measured from the box's own corner (`Oa`, `Ob`) with no side drawn out (`Drawn_Out => False`): **`Plan_Lot` given neither is exactly as before; the eight reference logs prove it.**
  - **A slanting box's ends run through the middles of blocks**: decide such a column one way everywhere (`Box_Edge_M`: at its beginning the box's, at its end not), in `For_Box_Columns` and in the bounds handed to `Plan_Lot` alike, or thirteen to twenty-nine columns go by which way a sum of floats falls and a neighbour's edge reads as an overlap.
  - **Its path is laid only where no stretch lies along its line** (`Path_Gaps`, a block by its middle: at a joint between two stretches of one long path it is one's or the other's); `Lay_Path` is called for each part left. A stretch's end is an end of a path only if no other stretch of that line joins it there (`Joint`): a slanting path's joints are not on corners.
  - **The zone keeps its frame** (`Zone_Rec.Placed`, `Fx`, `Fy`, `Way`, `Along_B`; `Zone_View.Placed`, `From`, `Way`, `Along_B`; blob 42, 30 to 41 still read): anything that moves a zone's record moves it whole (`Join_Plan` does). `Set_Box_Place` (`--no-box-place`; held off in `colony_check`'s and `pick_check`'s `Reset`): the player's tool; the scripts' `--stockpile` two corners are as they were and `--stockpile-box X Y WAY` is the new way. `colony_run --box-stats` counts a placed zone's block columns by whose they are.
  - **A tree in a stockpile put down whole is felled** (his "fell the tree in the stockpile"; `Set_Box_Fell`, `--no-box-fell`, not saved): `Plan_Lot`'s `Fell_Trees` takes the ground under a foot (never under a bough alone) and lays a place over it; the place is `Closed` while the tree stands (`Cell_Has_Trunk`), opened by `Box_Tree_Felled` from `Fell` (not when `Stores_Moved`); `Go_Fell_Box` is an extra task in `Think_Build`, before the paths; `Box_Trees_Standing` is counted once a colony second at the most, of the trees within the stockpiles' own columns (everyone who thinks asks it). **`Think` calls `Think_Build` only on a list of reasons** (the camp or its seats to build, a path to lay, earth in hand, the cart to pull, a tree or stump to take out of a stockpile): an extra task with no reason there is never begun once the camp, the paths and the cart are done (found after gate 71: a stockpile put down along a laid path kept its tree for six days; the checks had always had a path to lay). **A stump is taken out too** (`Box_Stump`: a tree felled by a forester before the stockpile was put down; three days in, the trees nearest the fire are stumps, and the places over them stayed closed for good): `Go_Fell_Box` goes to it, `Step_Wood`'s `Chopping` takes it out by `Stump_Out` and calls `Box_Tree_Felled`. `tools/square_sites.sh` saves with trees standing in stockpiles and runs on (the count decides whether anyone turns to the building, so a count that a load made differ would show). **The path tool's path goes through housing** (his "a paths pull through housing should give way as built, if the path can pass and not interfere with anything built"; `Lay_Path_Through`, called by the `Lay_Path` order; `Set_Path_Through`, `--no-path-through`, off in `colony_check`'s `Reset`): `Housing_Gives_Way`, never a hovel's lot (`Through_Lot`), any other zone as before; `Lay_Path` itself is untouched.
  - **The scripted pointer runs before there is a colony** only in a game that no option founds one in or loads one into (from when the view has filled): `--pointer "100:tool stake, 110:turn 1, 120:ground X Y, 150:click"` founds one by the tool (aim at a block whose middle rounds to the corner wanted: X - 0.1, Y - 0.1 for a middle at X, Y; with `--save-at FRAME FILE` the colony can be run on in the runner, `--box-stats` saying how its floor, hearth and path stand). **Try a new placement by its click in the game, in debug too, and save it half built and run it on (`$S/half.sh`: the straight run's state), before its gate**: the first stills of the square stopped at the hover. A switch a hand-founded game must obey is set where the option is read (`--no-square`, `--no-box-place`), not in `Castle.Scenario.Apply`, which runs only when a scenario founds the colony.

*From the entry "Building and zoning, a pass after stage 6a" (in `housing.md`):*

  - **A path is shown by the block columns it will turn** (`For_Path_Left`, `For_Path_Line`, `Castle.Zone_Marks.Show_Paths` and `Show_Path_Line`), and the pieces of a line are worked out by one routine for `Lay_Path` and for the line shown (`Line_Pieces`, `Line_Piece`). A mark that spans more than a block column is level across and straight in height along its length: anything shown on uneven ground is drawn column by column.

- Paths planned and laid, and no limit on how far (2026-11-10; `docs/handoffs/Handoff_2026_11_10_paths_and_reach.md` has his words). Accepted as built: first values, his to tune; don't treat one as settled against him, and don't change one unasked. The calls shown to him and not taken up stand by default (the handoff lists them). In the code:
  - **A path is a plan first.** `Lay_Path` records it (as stretches of `Path_Stretch_M`); `On_Path`/`Near_Path` are true of it at once, so zoning never waits for the laying. What is still to lay is never stored: `For_Path_Work` reads it from the ground (inside `Advance`, once a second: `Refresh_Paths`), `Lay_Metre` writes a metre at once. Done-test: nothing but `For_Path_Work` decides whether a block of path is to be turned.
  - **Looking further is one state**, `Far_Reach` (saved): `Look_Further` widens it a ring at a time, only when the ground so far is in (`Ground_In`) and looked through (`Looked_Through`); `Look_Near_Again` drops it. Only the camp's building asks (`Think_Build`); ordinary work keeps `Work_Radius_M`. A far stack is indexed without its bushes (`Index_Stack (..., Far => True)`): don't make far indexing full again (it tripled the save).
  - `--stone-from M` (`Set_Stone_From`) is the measuring start that sends the band afield on a world where stone is never far; `--paths-built` lays planned paths on the spot.
  - The eating rules were restated by John in the same answer and are unchanged: don't move `Hungry_At` (40 %) or `Very_Hungry` (15 %, when a member fends) unasked.
